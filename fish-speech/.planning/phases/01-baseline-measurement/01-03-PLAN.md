      - `f0_cv`: coefficient of variation (std / mean)

3. **Compute corpus-wide aggregates**:
   - Mean and std of per-clip f0_mean_hz
   - Mean and std of per-clip f0_std_hz
   - Mean f0_cv across clips
   - Distribution of contour_shape categories (count of flat/moderate/expressive)

**Part A: Create `tools/tts_baseline/analyze_pauses.py`**

1. **Accepts CLI args**: `--input-dir PATH` (default: `/home/prana/tts-test/outputs/baseline_corpus`), `--output PATH` (default: `pause_analysis.json` in input dir)
2. **For each WAV file**:
   a. Load with soundfile: `data, sr = sf.read(path)`
   b. Compute RMS energy in 10ms frames:
      ```python
      frame_size = int(sr * 0.010)  # 441 samples at 44100Hz
      hop_size = frame_size  # non-overlapping
      rms = np.array([np.sqrt(np.mean(data[i:i+frame_size]**2)) for i in range(0, len(data) - frame_size, hop_size)])
      ```
   c. Determine silence threshold: `threshold = np.percentile(rms[rms > 0], 10)` (10th percentile of non-zero RMS, catching the quietest speech). If this is too aggressive, use `0.01` as a floor.
   d. Detect pauses: contiguous runs of frames where `rms < threshold` and duration >= 100ms (ignore micro-gaps < 100ms that are just consonant closures)
   e. For each detected pause, record:
      - `start_s`: start time in seconds
      - `end_s`: end time in seconds
      - `duration_ms`: duration in milliseconds
      - `location`: "beginning" (first 10% of audio), "middle" (10-90%), "end" (last 10%)
   f. Compute per-clip statistics:
      - `pause_count`: number of pauses >= 100ms
      - `total_pause_duration_ms`: sum of all pause durations
      - `mean_pause_ms`: mean pause duration
      - `max_pause_ms`: longest pause
      - `min_pause_ms`: shortest pause
      - `pause_rate_per_second`: pause_count / audio_duration
      - `pause_locations`: {"beginning": N, "middle": N, "end": N}
3. **Compute corpus-wide aggregates**:
   - Mean and std of pause_count per clip
   - Mean and std of mean_pause_ms
   - Overall pause duration histogram buckets: [100-200ms, 200-400ms, 400-600ms, 600-800ms, 800ms+]
   - Pause location distribution aggregated across all clips
4. **Save** to `pause_analysis.json` with `per_clip` and `corpus_summary` sections (same structure as f0_analysis.json)
